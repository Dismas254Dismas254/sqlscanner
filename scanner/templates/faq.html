Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of the most common questions about our Vulnerability Scanner.

What is a vulnerability scan?

A vulnerability scan is a process used to identify potential security flaws in a website or web application that could be exploited by malicious actors.

How do I use the vulnerability scanner?

Simply register, log in, and follow the instructions to scan your website for vulnerabilities. The results will provide a report with recommendations for improving security.

What types of vulnerabilities are detected?

We primarily detect all types of SQL Injection (SQLi) vulnerabilities.

Can I get support if I encounter issues?

Yes! You can reach out to us via the "Help" section for email or WhatsApp support.
